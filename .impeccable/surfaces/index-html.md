# Surface: rivkahaim.com (index.html, all hash routes)

Mode: Experience with a selling close. Audience: private art buyers (Hebrew first), arriving from Instagram, often on phone. Action: enquire about a specific, numbered work via WhatsApp. Proof on hand: the works themselves, her bio, studio photos. No prices, testimonials or press exist; none invented.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A classic gallery site (Zwirner-calibre restraint) rendered in warm earth paper instead of white wall: the work hangs large, labels are museum-precise, and buying is a private conversation with the artist. Refuses the dark-veil-over-photo hero and gold hairlines of the old site.
OWN-WORLD: unbleached-paper ground (#E8DECD) with a faint fibre grain, espresso ink (#2A1F17), umber and kraft mid-tones, deep espresso bands (#2B2019) for the artist and the close. Bellefair display, Assistant text. Museum wall labels (No., title, medium, year, availability) under every work. Square, quiet buttons; no rules-as-decoration, no cards with shadows.
STORY: See a beautiful original at full size, learn it is one of a kind and handmade from paper, find the piece you want by its number, message Rivka about it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop split, ~58% "Stripes of Colour" (lp-08) photographed on the wall, full height; ~42% text column: small label "Original works in paper", H1 "Rivka Haim" large, one line of positioning, primary "View the collection", secondary "Private enquiry", and a museum label for the pictured work beneath. Mobile: image first, text below.
FORM: canon (category standard), user-chosen and steered warm/earth; seed key 30e3d34e. Signature interaction: work detail with lettered views (A/B/C) and a sticky label column whose enquiry pre-fills the work's number and title.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
