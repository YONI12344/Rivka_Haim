# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: private art buyers (Hebrew-speaking first, English second) looking for an original, handmade piece for their home or as a gift. They arrive from Instagram (@rivkahaim) or word of mouth, often on a phone, and decide whether to message Rivka about a specific work.
Secondary: people looking for a studio workshop (weekly Sculpture Group, Paper & Wine events for parties, birthdays, bachelorettes).

## Product Purpose
The personal site of Rivka Haim, a paper artist. It presents her original works and turns interest into a private enquiry about a specific piece (via WhatsApp). Success = a visitor messages about a named work. Workshops and student work are secondary paths.

## Positioning
Every piece is an original made by hand by Rivka from paper, much of it recycled or upcycled (bags, pages, everyday paper). Two bodies of work: "Liquid Paper" (נייר נוזלי) framed wall works, and hand-built paper sculptures and objects. Made in her garden studio in Moshav Sha'ar Efraim.

## Operating Context
- Bilingual Hebrew (RTL, default) / English (LTR) with a toggle remembered in localStorage.
- All enquiries go to WhatsApp 052-324-5039 with a prefilled message naming the work; also Instagram @rivkahaim and email rivkahaim5@gmail.com.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages at rivkahaim.com (repo YONI12344/Rivka_Haim, branch main; push = live).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single static `index.html` (inline CSS/JS, hash router) plus `images/`. No build step, no backend.
- Selling model (confirmed 2026-09-30): works are presented as available originals, price on request. No prices shown. Enquiry button per work.
- Artwork titles are shown (confirmed 2026-09-30).
- Dimensions are known only for "Garden in Paper" (60 × 80 cm). Do not invent dimensions, prices, or edition info.
- Studio address is not published on the contact page (deliberate removal); the moshav name appears only in the studio story.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: רבקה חיים / Rivka Haim. Title: "Paper Artist" / "יוצרת בנייר".
- Quote: "לכל פיסת נייר יש חיים שלמים שמחכים להתגלות" / "Every piece of paper has a whole life waiting to be discovered".
- Rivka's bio text on the About page is hers; keep its substance.

## Evidence on Hand

- 9 student pieces (images/gallery/students-*).
- Portraits and studio photos (images/portrait/), workshop photos (images/workshops/).
- No testimonials, press, exhibitions, collectors or prices on hand. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. The artwork leads; the interface stays quiet around it.
2. Every work page ends in one clear, personal enquiry about that piece.
3. Handmade and original is the value; say it truthfully, never inflate it.
4. Hebrew is the first language, not a translation.
